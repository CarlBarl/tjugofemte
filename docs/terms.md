# Tjugofemte terms of use

Tjugofemte is free, open source software provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It is a budgeting aid, not financial advice, and the numbers it shows depend on the categorization rules you set and the data your bank returns.

You connect your bank through an Enable Banking application that you register and own. Enable Banking's terms of service govern that application; in particular, their free production mode is for the personal use of private individuals and their own accounts.

The author of Tjugofemte does not operate any service, does not receive your data and cannot restore it. Keep your own records if you need them; the app can export CSV at any time.

The source code is available under the MIT licence in the public repository.
